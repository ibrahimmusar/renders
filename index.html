About

This conference brings together researchers, academicians, and professionals in mathematics, computer science, and interdisciplinary fields to address global sustainability challenges. It highlights how mathematical and computational tools contribute to real-world solutions aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Over two days, participants will exchange ideas, present research, and explore collaborations. Topics include mathematical modeling, AI, machine learning, and computational techniques, with applications in climate change, resource management, and public health. Discussions will also cover the evolving role of traditional mathematical approaches.

This conference supports young researchers, offering them a platform to present work, receive feedback, and connect with experts, fostering innovation and advancing sustainable development.
